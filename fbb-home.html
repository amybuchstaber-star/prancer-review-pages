01 — The Method

The 3 / 60 / 10K system

The same weekly structure Marcus used to compete at the Games for 6 straight years — scaled for real adults with real schedules.

3

Strength sessions / week

Three lifting days. Every body area hit twice with two tough sets — the 2×2 Rule. Tempo-based work that builds muscle and protects joints.

60

Minutes cardio / week

Total — not 5 hours of zone 2. Just enough aerobic base to live well without grinding down your recovery.

10K

Steps / day

The most underrated longevity move on earth. Free, automatic, and the glue that holds the whole system together.
