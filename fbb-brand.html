02 · Voice

The coach, not the hype man

FBB sounds like a coach with nothing to prove. Direct sentences. Specific numbers. Training vocabulary used naturally, with a dry warmth that earns trust.

We say

"Three lifting days. Every body area twice." · "Get built, not burnt." · "Costs less than one massage." · Tempo, sets, tracks, quality reps. Prescriptive, specific, plain.

We never say

Crush, grind, beast mode, no excuses · "Get shredded in 30 days" · exclamation points doing the work of evidence · two ideas in one sentence.

The story voice

When Marcus speaks in first person (the burnout, the comeback, the why) the type shifts to the italic. The system talks in caps; the human talks in italic lowercase — same family, different posture.

Rule · teal acts

Teal is the color of doing: buttons, links, play controls. If it's teal, you can press it. It never decorates.

Rule · flame pops

Flame carries the heat: the accent word in a headline, the key stat, a flag, the big method numbers, the star rating. Several small pops per screen are welcome. What flame never does: fill large areas or become a button.

Rule · the family note

FBB and Staple are siblings, not twins. Staple's sunshine yellow and geometric blocks appear in exactly one place on FBB pages: the Staple cross-brand card. Nowhere else.

05 · Emphasis

Chalk, not bold-scatter

One idea per passage gets one mark. On dark, a dusty chalk swipe: the gym's own highlighter. On light, teal chalk or a shift into the italic. Scattered bolding is retired.

Chalk swipe

The method works in three hours a week. The swipe goes behind the one phrase that closes the argument.

Chalk underline

Numbers get underlined like a coach circling the whiteboard: every body area, twice.

Italic shift

Or the sentence simply changes voice, because the human aside is the emphasis.

The rule

One emphasis per paragraph. If everything is highlighted, nothing is. (This sheet practices what it preaches.)

06 · Visual storytelling

Chapters, not sections

The scroll reads like a training film: full-bleed duotone photography, a giant chapter numeral, one italic line over the image. This is the move that makes FBB feel premium instead of templated.

Duotone spec

Photo → grayscale, contrast +6% → teal color-blend at 25–30% → ink gradient from the bottom. Warm skin survives; the frame feels cinematic and unmistakably FBB.

The water

Water is FBB's recurring element. Marcus trains beside the bay, and the brand moves like it: a slow-drifting waveline marks the passage into each chapter, the teal wash pools faintly behind light sections, and shorelines anchor the photography. Movement, recovery, the long run. The water carries all three.

The numeral

Chapter numbers set huge and outlined, bleeding off the corner. They give the eye a landmark at every beat of the scroll. Wayfinding as art direction.

One line over the image

Never a paragraph on a photo. One italic line, one attribution, one optional session tag. The image does the storytelling; the words caption it.

07 · Photography

Real coach, real light

What we show

Marcus training, coaching, writing programming; members' honest befores and afters. Natural light: the beach, the garage gym, the whiteboard. Never stock, never studio muscle shots.

Two treatments only

Dark chapters get a soft teal grade, never a heavy crush. Light water chapters run natural: calm bay, soft sky, premium tools in frame. The feel is coastal and premium, never tropical or beachy-cheap. Supporting photos run natural color in thin ink frames.

Hierarchy

One hero image per section; everything else supports at half scale. When a photo and a headline compete, the photo loses.

Motion coaches

Things move when they demonstrate or point: chalk drawing itself under a phrase, a number counting up, a film playing, the waveline drifting at walking pace. Decorative drift is capped at one element per section, and reduced-motion is always respected.

Video is proof

Click-to-play, never autoplay with sound. The hero film, member testimonials, movement demos: each appears at its beat in the story, not scattered through the page.

One path, three echoes

Every page funnels to one action, the free 14-day Persist trial, at beats 1, 5 and 8, always teal, always the same words. E-books and Coaching sell only inside the offer section. Everything else is a quiet underline.
