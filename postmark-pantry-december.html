Questions, answered

Everything you might be wondering.

Taste, texture, tummy talk, and shipping.

Still curious about something? Drop us a line.

Wait, how does a powder turn into real jam?

Just scoop Jam Pact into a little bowl or jar, pour in 4 ounces of tart cherry or pomegranate juice (plus a little honey or maple if you want it sweeter), and stir well. Within two minutes, the whole-food psyllium and chia soak up the juice and thicken into lush, spreadable jam. No cooking, no stovetop, no gelatin or fake thickeners.

What should I mix it with? Can I just use water?

Dark red juices like tart cherry, pomegranate, or cranberry blend are our absolute favorites. They give you that rich jewel color and classic jammy flavor. Plain water totally works in a pinch! It will just taste milder and a little earthy-tart from the baobab and apple, so definitely stir in a spoonful of maple syrup or honey and maybe a mash of fresh berries. Stirring a scoop directly into yogurt or kefir is delicious, too.

What does it actually taste like?

It takes on the flavor of whatever juice you stir it with! Because we don't add any sugar or fake flavoring to the jar, you're in total control of the sweetness. Made with tart cherry juice and a touch of honey, it tastes like bright, tangy homemade cherry-berry preserves.

Is the texture gritty or slimy like regular fiber powders?

Not at all, and trust me, I couldn't stand choking down gritty orange fiber sludge either! Jam Pact sets up just like soft, spoonable raspberry or blackberry preserves, with that pleasant little seed crunch you get from chia. If it sits in the fridge overnight and gets extra thick, a quick splash of juice loosens it right back up.

What are the exact ingredients?

Organic psyllium husk, chia seed, baobab fruit, apple fiber, ground flaxseed, green banana flour, and turkey tail mushroom. Seven real plants delivering 11g of diverse soluble, insoluble, and prebiotic fiber per scoop. No gums, no synthetic isolates, no "natural flavors."

Will 11 grams of fiber make me bloated at first?

If your body isn't used to getting much fiber yet, jumping straight into the deep end can make your belly rumble! We always recommend starting with half a scoop for your first 5 to 7 days and drinking plenty of water alongside it so your gut microbiome can happily adjust. After a week, work right up to a full scoop.

Why pair Jam Pact with Pitcher Perfect?

Fiber works its magic by absorbing water in your digestive tract, so good hydration makes your fiber work ten times better. Jam Pact gives you 11g of whole-food fiber at breakfast, and Pitcher Perfect follows up in the afternoon with 4 key minerals, 0g sugar, and 5g of clear-dissolving prebiotic fiber. Together, they're a powerhouse for digestion, energy, and hydration.

How fast do you ship, and what if I don't love it?

We pack and ship orders within 2 to 4 business days in plastic-free packaging, complete with tissue paper and a wax-sealed note. Unopened jars can be returned within 30 days for a full refund, and if you open a jar and genuinely aren't loving it, email us anyway so we can help make it right.

Bake & prep with Jam Pact.

Whip up a quick jar with juice first, then bake and layer with it just like store-bought preserves.

35 minutes · makes 24 cookies

Tart cherry thumbprint cookies

Buttery shortbread rounds cradling ruby-red pools of Jam Pact in the center.

How to make them
  1. Cream 1 cup softened butter with 1/2 cup brown sugar, then beat in 1 egg yolk and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract.
  2. Fold in 2 cups flour and a good pinch of salt. Roll into bite-sized balls and press your thumb gently into the middle of each.
  3. Bake at 350°F for 10 minutes, spoon prepared Jam Pact into each well, and bake 5 minutes longer.
45 minutes · makes 16 squares

Brown-butter oat jam bars

Toasty cinnamon oat crumble sandwiched around a thick, jammy berry middle.

How to make them
  1. Mix 1 1/2 cups rolled oats, 1 cup flour, 2/3 cup brown sugar, 3/4 cup cold cubed butter, and 1 teaspoon cinnamon with your fingers until crumbly.
  2. Press two-thirds of the mixture firmly into a parchment-lined 8-inch pan and spread a prepared jar of Jam Pact evenly across it.
  3. Scatter the remaining crumble over the top and bake at 350°F for 30 to 35 minutes until golden. Cool completely before slicing.
5 minutes · makes 2 jars

Grab-and-go yogurt parfaits

Creamy Greek yogurt, crunchy granola, and ribbons of Jam Pact prepped ahead for busy mornings.

How to make them
  1. Spoon thick Greek or coconut yogurt into two small Mason jars.
  2. Layer in a generous spoonful of prepared Jam Pact and a handful of your favorite granola.
  3. Repeat the layers, top with fresh berries, and tuck into the fridge for tomorrow morning.

Chapter I

The overflowing supplement cabinet

Open almost any woman's kitchen cabinet right now and you'll see the exact same thing: a graveyard of half-empty supplement bottles, hormone powders, greens blends, and pricey gummies we bought hoping this one would finally make us feel good again.

Yet almost every woman I talk to is dealing with the exact same frustrations. You feel sluggish, your hormones are all over the map, your stomach feels heavy, you aren't going to the bathroom regularly, and you're spending a small fortune on wellness routines you forget to take by Thursday.

Here's the truth nobody in the supplement aisle wants to admit: if your digestive system is backed up and your gut lining is compromised, expensive supplements are basically putting lipstick on a pig. You can't absorb nutrients, or clear out excess hormones, when the foundation is broken.
The bottom line

Without a healthy gut, nothing else sticks.

Chapter II

Why fiber changes everything.

When we stepped back from the wellness noise and looked at what women are genuinely missing every single day, it wasn't another isolated vitamin or trendy adaptogen. It was fiber. Women need at least 28 grams of diverse fiber a day to thrive, and over 90% of us aren't even coming close.

Fiber isn't sexy, so brands don't hype it up. Yet fiber is the quiet workhorse behind almost everything we're chasing! It feeds the good bacteria that heal and protect your gut barrier. It binds to excess hormones like estrogen so your body can actually clear them out instead of recirculating them. It steadies blood sugar so you aren't crashing at 3:00 PM, keeps you comfortably regular every single day, and unlocks your body's ability to absorb the good food you eat.

So instead of inventing another capsule to swallow, we put whole-food fiber right back where it belongs: on warm buttered toast and in a cold, refreshing glass of lemonade.

Three toasts for the first chilly Saturday.

by Amy · 4-minute read · October, from my kitchen

You know that first crisp Saturday in October when the house feels nippy in the morning and turning on the toaster feels like an event? This little menu was made for that exact morning. Grab one mixed jar of Jam Pact, slice up a good loaf of bread, and try one (or all three) of these.

01 · The Purist

Thick-sliced bakery sourdough, toasted just a tiny bit darker than golden. Real salted butter melted straight into the nooks while it's piping hot, topped with tart cherry Jam Pact spread right out to the crust. Always go all the way to the crust. Best eaten leaning against the kitchen counter while your coffee cools down.

02 · The Tangy Swirl

Spread a thick cloud of whole-milk Greek yogurt or cottage cheese onto toasted seeded rye, then spoon pomegranate Jam Pact generously over the top so the ruby juices streak down the sides. It looks straight out of a neighborhood cafe and casually hands you 11g of fiber plus protein in three minutes.

03 · The Weekend Host

Creamy ricotta whipped with a fork, a lush layer of Jam Pact, fresh grated lemon zest, and a pinch of flaky sea salt. Make this one when your sister or best friend sleeps over. It takes ninety seconds and everyone acts like you went to culinary school.

Tip: Mix your Jam Pact in a cute jar the night before, or stir it together right before you push the lever down on the toaster. By the time the bread pops, your jam is ready.

How does Jam Pact turn into real jam without cooking?

Stir one scoop into 4 ounces of dark red juice (tart cherry and pomegranate are our go-tos!) plus a drizzle of honey or maple syrup if you like. Wait two minutes while the whole-food psyllium and chia naturally soak up the juice and thicken into lush, spreadable preserves. Zero stovetop, zero added pectin, zero weirdness.

What does Jam Pact taste like?

Bright, fruity, and pleasantly tart, just like homemade berry preserves! Because we don't put sugar or fake flavorings in the blend, it takes on the delicious flavor of whatever juice you mix it with, and you control how sweet you want it.

Is it safe for kids (and pregnant or nursing moms)?

Jam Pact is made strictly from seven whole plants. No laxative herbs, no artificial sweeteners, no junk, so kids love it as everyday jam! Because kids have smaller tummies, start them with just a teaspoon or two of prepared jam. If you are pregnant, nursing, or managing a medical condition, always do a quick check-in with your doctor or midwife first, especially since Jam Pact includes turkey tail mushroom.

How much fiber am I getting in each jar?

Jam Pact packs 11g of whole-food soluble, insoluble, and prebiotic fiber per scoop. Pitcher Perfect delivers 5g of clear-dissolving soluble fiber alongside 4 essential minerals, making it the rare electrolyte drink that supports your gut too.

Will adding this much fiber make me gassy or bloated?

Whenever you wake up a fiber-starved gut, going slowly is key! Start with half a scoop for your first week and drink plenty of water so your microbiome adjusts smoothly. Once your body settles in, you'll feel lighter, comfortable, and reliably regular.
