Questions

Before you ask

What equipment do I need?

A standard gym covers every track. Training at home? The Minimalist track runs on dumbbells and a bench, and most other tracks include equipment substitutions.

I'm over 40 and have old injuries. Is this for me?

You're the exact person it was built for. Tempo work, unilateral strength and sensible volume protect joints while still building muscle. Thousands of members train around old injuries.

How long are the sessions?

Most sessions run 45 to 60 minutes, three days a week, with optional extras if you want more. The whole method fits in about three hours a week.

Can I switch tracks?

Anytime, inside the app, without losing your history. Members switch for travel, new gyms and new goals constantly. That's the point.

What if it's not for me?

The first 14 days are free, and there's a 30-day money-back guarantee after that. If it's not working, you're out nothing.
