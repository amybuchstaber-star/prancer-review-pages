The gap,
in food.

The benchmark is 14 grams of fiber for every 1,000 calories. Pick your day, then catch the foods that get you there. Every food shows its real fiber grams.

Lentils + an apple = 12.2 g.That covers the average gap. Start there.
Chicken + eggs = 0 g.The protein plate is where the fiber number goes missing.
Your number moves with your day.2,000 calories is 28 g. 2,500 is 35 g. 3,000 is 42 g.

Food values: USDA FoodData Central, SR Legacy. Benchmark of 14 g per 1,000 calories: 81 FR 33742. An estimate for healthy adults, not medical advice. The scoop power-up is for fun and is not a claim about STÄPL.
