the practical stuff

shipping & returns

Everything you need to know about getting your goodies — and what to do if something isn't right.

Shipping

Every piece is handmade, so please allow 2–4 business days for us to pack up your order. Once it ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with tracking. Orders ship from Minnesota via USPS; most arrive within 3–7 business days. We currently ship within the United States.

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on your order. If your package arrives damaged, email us within 7 days at hello@cocoandkal.com with a photo and we'll make it right.

Returns & exchanges

We want you to love what you ordered. If you don't, you may return unworn, unused items in their original condition within 30 days of delivery for a refund to your original payment method. Return shipping is the buyer's responsibility unless the item arrived damaged or we made a mistake.

To start a return or exchange, email hello@cocoandkal.com with your order number. Custom and made-to-order pieces are final sale unless they arrive damaged.

Refunds are issued within 5–10 business days of us receiving your return.

your info, kept safe

privacy policy

Coco & Kal, LLC (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

When you place an order or contact us, we collect the information you provide: your name, email address, shipping address, and order details. Payment is processed securely by our payment provider — we never see or store your full card number. Like most websites, we may also collect basic analytics (pages visited, device type) to understand how the site is used.

How we use it

We use your information to fulfill and ship orders, respond to your messages, and — only if you've signed up — send occasional newsletter emails (you can unsubscribe any time). We never sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share information only with the services that help us run the shop — our e-commerce platform, payment processor, and shipping carriers — and only what they need to do their job.

Your choices

You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing hello@cocoandkal.com.
