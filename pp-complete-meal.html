Eating less? Make every bite count.

protein has to be efficient.

If you're taking in fewer calories — by choice, by schedule, or with a doctor's help — the goal is simple: get enough protein to protect muscle, and enough fat to feel fed, without spending calories on anything else.

A smaller appetite makes 100+ grams of protein a day hard to reach with plates of food. Two scoops deliver 39 grams in a glass, so one small meal carries its weight.

Fasting windows and single-meal days work better when the meal you do have is dense. This is the one you can make in 30 seconds and drink on the way out the door.

Questions

Before you scoop.

Is 300 calories really a meal?

For a lot of people, yes — especially if you're eating less right now. What makes it a meal isn't the calorie count, it's the composition: 39 grams of complete protein, 14 grams of fat, and the vitamins and minerals from five organs and bone broth. That's the profile of a plate of steak with a side of liver, minus the plate. If you need more, add a banana, some berries, or a spoon of nut butter to the blender.

Can I take this on a GLP-1 medication?

Paleo Pro products are food — beef, egg, organs, tallow, bone broth — so there's nothing in them that interacts with medication. Many customers on these medications use the duo because it's a lot of protein in a small volume, which is exactly what a reduced appetite calls for.

Follow your doctor's guidance on how much protein and how many calories you should be eating each day. This is one easy way to hit that target, not a substitute for the plan they gave you.

Do I have to use both every time?

No. Plenty of people use Beef & Egg on its own after a workout and save the two-scoop version for a meal. The duo is the meal; the single scoop is the snack. Buy both and you get to choose.

What does it taste like?

Like a chocolate or vanilla shake. Carnivore Complete's organs are freeze-dried and fully blended — customers who can't stomach liver on a plate tell us they'd never know it was in there. Cacao with Aztec Vanilla makes a chocolate-vanilla shake; cacao with cacao is full chocolate. Mayan Mocha Beef & Egg with Carnivore Complete in cold brew is a whole breakfast.

How long does a bag last?

Each 1 lb bag is 15 scoops. One bag of each gives you 15 complete meals — a little over two weeks at one a day. Subscribe & Save ships on the cadence you choose (every 2, 4, 8 or 12 weeks) at 15% off, and you can pause, skip, or cancel anytime.
