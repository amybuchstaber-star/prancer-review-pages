Fair questions

What does "complete protein" actually mean? +

A complete protein contains all nine essential amino acids — the ones your body can't make on its own. Beef protein isolate covers most of them; egg white protein fills in the rest. Together, one scoop delivers the full profile your body needs to build and keep muscle.

Why does protein matter more after 45? +

Research shows the body becomes less efficient at turning protein into muscle over time — the same meal simply does less. The fix isn't eating more of everything; it's making each meal count, with a meaningful 20–30 grams of protein at a time. One scoop delivers 26 grams at just 120 calories.

Is this a good companion to GLP-1 medications? +

Many people on GLP-1 medications find it hard to eat enough protein with a smaller appetite — and protein is exactly what helps you lose weight without losing strength. 26 grams of complete protein at 120 calories fits a suppressed appetite well. As always, talk with your doctor about your specific plan.

Will it upset my stomach? +

The three most common culprits — whey, lactose, and soy — aren't in the bag.
