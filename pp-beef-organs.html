Questions

Before you switch.

Are these really the same as the $50 organ supplements?

The organs are the same category of ingredient: freeze-dried, grass-fed beef liver, heart, kidney, spleen and pancreas from pasture-raised cattle, in a gelatin capsule with nothing else. That's what most premium multi-organ brands sell, and it's what we sell. The clearest way to check is to hold the two labels side by side — organ list, per-organ amounts, "other ingredients" line, and price. Ours puts every organ and every milligram on the label.

Then why is yours less expensive?

Because we've been making it since 2011 and we spend on testing, not on marketing. No celebrity founder, no sponsored-post budget, no premium packaging to pay for. The organ costs what the organ costs; the rest is up to the brand.

How many capsules do I take?

One serving is six capsules, once a day — most people take them with breakfast. That's 3.6 grams of the five-organ blend per serving, 30 servings per bottle.

Do they taste or smell like organs?

No. The organs are freeze-dried into a powder and capsuled, so there's no taste, no cooking and no "organ face." If you'd rather drink your organs, Carnivore Complete puts the same five in a vanilla protein shake.

Where are the organs from?

Grass-fed, pasture-raised cattle in New Zealand and Argentina — the two places most of the world's grass-fed beef organ supply comes from, ours included. They're freeze-dried and encapsulated in the USA, and every batch is tested by Light Labs for lead, arsenic, cadmium and mercury with the results published.

What if it isn't for me?

Sixty days, money back. Write to info@paleopro.com and we'll refund you. No return shipping games, no restocking fine print.
