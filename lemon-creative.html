The moments you'll miss
Isn't that the photographer's job?

Not the same thing, I promise

Your photographer is building a polished gallery. Your videographer is crafting a film. Both are worth every penny — and both take weeks. I'm the third person: phone in hand, capturing the unscripted stuff as it happens and sending it to you by the next morning so you have something to post on the honeymoon.

  • Getting ready, first looks, vows, toasts, dance floor — all of it, candid.
  • Vertical video shot for Instagram & TikTok, not cropped from a cinema camera.
  • Edited highlight reels with trending audio, ready to post.
